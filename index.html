Crank Lab

A build-fight practice range. Snappy Chapter 1-style running and jumping, turbo build on a 5 m by 4 m grid, and wall, floor, ramp and cone edits. Ramp rush, crank 90s, and chase your best height.

Keybinds

Click a bind, then press any key, mouse button (side buttons work) or scroll the wheel. Esc cancels. Taken binds swap.

Wall / Floor / Ramp / Cone

Edit
Aim at a build, tap Edit, then drag over tiles and lift your finger. A wrong pattern turns red until you fix it
Fire
Place the current piece, or swing the pickaxe

Ramp edits: select the two tiles on the side you want the ramp to rise toward. Cone edits: each selected tile raises that corner to the full height of the wall, so two side tiles make a ramp that reaches the top, one tile makes a corner roof, and two diagonal tiles make a ridge.
