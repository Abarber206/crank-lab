    Keybinds

    Click a bind, then press any key, mouse button (side buttons work) or scroll the wheel. Esc cancels. Taken binds swap.

    Left side
    Drag to move
    Right side

    Wall / Floor / Ramp / Cone
    Tap to place, hold to turbo build
    Edit
    Aim at a build, tap Edit, then drag over tiles and lift your finger. A wrong pattern turns red until you fix it
    Slot bar
    Tap a weapon to equip it. In build mode, tap a piece to place it, hold to turbo
    Hammer
    Switch between weapons and build mode
    Fire
    Shoot, place the current piece, or swing the pickaxe

    Controller: A jump, R3 crouch, RT fire, RB/LB switch weapon, X reload, B build mode, Y edit. In build mode RT wall, RB floor, LT stairs, LB cone (hold to turbo), X rotate. In edit, RT selects tiles, Y confirms, B resets. D-pad right switches material.

    Stair edits: drag across two side-by-side tiles for a half stair that rises from the first tile you touched, or all four to re-aim the full stair. Cone edits: each selected tile raises that corner to the full height of the wall, so two side tiles make a ramp that reaches the top, one tile makes a corner roof, and two diagonal tiles make a ridge.
